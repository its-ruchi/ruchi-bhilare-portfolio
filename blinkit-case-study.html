THE BIG IDEA

People don’t stay loyal to delivery apps. They stay out of habit.

So every “out of stock” moment is a free trial for a rival app. The rival didn’t spend a rupee on ads. Blinkit’s empty shelf did the marketing for them.

In stockMissing
Was this just my mom?

Blinkit is actually the best in India at keeping things in stock. In a study of 150 everyday items, it had them about 94 times out of 100. Zepto: about 82.

But Blinkit delivers around 25 lakh orders every day. Even 6 missing items in 100 adds up to a lot of “no milk” mornings.

Sources: Bernstein study, via Business Today; Zepto’s IPO filing, via StartupFeed (both Oct 2026).

7,500people a day start making the rival app their habit. That’s 27 lakh a year.

WHAT I’D FIND OUT FIRST

  • How many “out of stock” moments really end with someone opening another app?
  • Do people trust an automatic swap, or want to say yes each time?
  • Should “Only 3 left” show to everyone, or only to people who buy that item?
