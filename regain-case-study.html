FIRST IMPRESSION OF THE APP

Regain Case Study: UI Research

Hi, I’m Ruchi. I downloaded Regain and wrote down every mistake I noticed in my first three minutes. Honestly.
Tap anywhere, or press the → key

Independent UI research. Not affiliated with Regain.

THE BIG IDEA

First impression is the last impression.

People download a focus app because they want to start focusing. Every screen before that is a chance for them to give up and close it.

Nothing moves

MISTAKE 1

The logo just sits there.

This is the first thing I saw when I opened the app: an hourglass logo on a green screen. It didn’t move at all.

I couldn’t tell if it was loading or stuck.

My real screenshot from the app.

No mute button anywhere

MISTAKE 2

The voice starts talking.

The mascot reads every question out loud. There’s no option to mute it anywhere on the screen.

If I’d opened this at the office, everyone would have heard it.

My real screenshot from the app.

No Skip

MISTAKE 3

You can’t skip onboarding.

It’s long, and every step is compulsory.

Some people downloaded Regain just for a Pomodoro timer. Making them do all of this first isn’t a good start.

Concept screen, drawn by me.

MISTAKE 4

It assumes I have competition to beat.

This screen told me “You are helping your competition get good grades.” But I’m not preparing for any exam.

What competition? I’m just studying for myself.

Not everyone has a specific goal or a rival. When the app assumes one, people like me feel this app isn’t for them.

My real screenshot from the app.

MISTAKE 5

Showing me how far I am from my goal.

In the first few minutes, onboarding builds scarcity. It shows how far away I am.

People already know they waste time. They know social media is built to hook them. They don’t need it confirmed. It made me less interested, not more.

And the copy: “Your dream is a long climb.” It could have just said “The mountain is you.”

MISTAKE 6

A 2-minute session started on its own.

Right after I gave permissions, the timer began. I didn’t choose it.

For the first few seconds, I couldn’t even see where to set my timer.

Concept screen, drawn by me.

So what would I change? Six things, and most of them are small.
FIX 1Moving hourglassThe hourglass logo drains and fills while the app loads
FIX 2Mute buttonOn the first screen, and it follows silent mode
FIX 3Skip onboardingGo straight to the timer, answer questions later
FIX 4“No goal” optionFor people who just want to focus
FIX 5Story laterOne line first, the full climb after the first session
FIX 6I set the timerA clear time picker and Start button, every time
4:01●●●

FIX 1

Make the hourglass move.

The logo is already an hourglass. Let the top drain into the bottom while the app loads, and open the next page when it’s full.

Now the wait feels like progress, and the logo finally does what it looks like it should.

FIXES 2, 3, 4 AND 6

The first screen I’d want.

Straight to a timer I set.

Mute and Skip are there from the first second, and there’s an answer for people without a big goal.

Tap “I just want a focus timer”

One tap, and I’m on the timer. I pick the time, I press Start. Setup questions can come later.

The mountain is you.

First step done.

FIX 5

Show the climb after the first win.

Keep the beautiful mountain, but show it after someone finishes their first session.

Then “You” is already one step up. It feels like progress, not pressure.
