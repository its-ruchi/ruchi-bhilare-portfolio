THE PRODUCT INSIGHT
Saving captures intent. The product should help turn that intent into action.
Saving takes one tap. Reading takes minutes, attention and a moment that never arrives on its own.
                     Assumptions to validate
  • Personal observation. I remember topics, not exact articles.

  • Assumption. Readers save more than they read.

  • Assumption. Forgetting, not lost interest, is the main cause.

  • To test. A gentle reminder beats none, without raising opt-outs.

The bigger picture
We are not just adding features. We are helping readers turn saved intent into real reading, while staying true to the simple, focused Substack experience.
What to test first
Start with the smallest experiment that tests the core user problem.
01
TEST FIRST
Reading queue
Make saved, unread articles visible and easy to resume.
Do more users return to read what they saved?
02
Contextual reminders
Resurface unread articles at useful moments.
Do reminders increase reading without annoying users?
03
Voice assistant
Help readers understand difficult articles through voice or text.
Do readers want explanations while reading?
04
Screenshot discovery
Find articles from screenshots of posts discovered elsewhere.
How often do readers struggle to find content discovered on other platforms?
Why this order? The reading queue tests the core problem with less implementation complexity. Reminders build on that foundation. AI features follow once user demand is clearer.
How I would validate it
We believe making saved articles visible and easy to resume will help readers finish what they saved. We are right if saved-to-read conversion rises without opt-outs rising.

PRIMARY METRIC
% of users who read at least one saved article within a defined period
SECONDARY
  • Saved-to-read conversion
  • Article completion
  • Repeat readership
GUARDRAILS
  • Notification opt-outs
  • Email unsubscribe rate
  • Reminder dismissals
DECISION RULES
Expand: more reading, guardrails hold.
Adjust: readers return, opt-outs rise.
Stop: no lift after iterating.
Help readers finish what they were already interested in.
BUILD FIRST
Reading queue + reminders
Make saved, unread articles visible in one place. Show reading time and let readers resume where they left off.
Decision
Tests the core problem without building complex AI features.
VALIDATE NEXT
Does it change behaviour?
Test whether readers return to saved articles and actually read them.
Metrics
Saved-to-read conversion, article completion, repeat readership.
Guardrail
Notification opt-outs and unsubscribes.
WHAT I'D LEARN NEXT
Understand why readers drop off
  • Do readers forget, run out of time, or lose interest?

  • When does a reminder feel helpful rather than intrusive?

  • Do they want help understanding articles, or simply finding time to read?
EXPAND IF VALIDATED
Make reading easier
If readers return but struggle with long or complex articles, test an optional voice assistant that explains key ideas and answers questions.
Decision
Expand only if research identifies a real need.
