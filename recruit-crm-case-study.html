THE BIG IDEA

Switching a CRM is a trust decision.

An agency owner on this page is thinking about moving their whole database: every candidate, client and note. Each small mismatch on the page gives them one more reason to wait another quarter.

Heading: Crelate Card: Bullhorn

MISTAKE 1

The heading says Crelate. The card says Bullhorn.

The “Switching from…” heading rotates through competitors. The card under it always says Bullhorn.

If they get this name wrong, will they get my data right?

This section is meant to make switching feel safe. That makes it the worst place for a copy-paste slip.

MISTAKE 2

Three answers to “How fast can I go live?”

The FAQ, the switching section and the numbers section each give a different time.

One day? Two weeks? 48 hours? Which one do I plan around?

When I get three answers, I believe none of them. And the sales team ends up answering it again on every demo call.

MISTAKE 5

Too many doors, too many numbers.

There’s a countdown bar, and “Book a demo” shows up 7 times on the homepage, next to Start free trial, Take a tour, Talk to a migration specialist and Save my seat.

The numbers move too: 5,000+ integrations in one place, +3,000 more in another, thousands in the FAQ.

I run a team of three. Which button is mine?

Concept screen, drawn by me from the real page.

So what would I change? Five things, and most of them are just words.
FIX 1Name the right toolThe card names the same competitor as the heading
FIX 2One timelineDay 1, 48 hours, 2 weeks, in the same words everywhere
FIX 3One agent list“Live now” and “Coming soon”, the same in every menu
FIX 4Tuck the chat awayFold it into its icon, with options for buyers only
FIX 5Two clear doorsOne trial button, one demo button, each saying who it’s for
QUICK WINStart with fixes 1 and 3They’re copy changes: the biggest trust gain for the least work.

→ SWITCHING FROM CRELATE?

Switch in days
We migrate your data for youCandidates, contacts, companies, notes, and history. Our team maps and imports every record from Crelate so nothing is left behind.

FIX 1

The card follows the heading.

When the heading says Crelate, the card says Crelate too. Tap a name to try it.

Now a Crelate user sees their own tool named twice, and the switch feels planned for them. If that’s hard to build, “from your current ATS” works too.

FIX 2

One timeline, said the same way everywhere.

The three numbers don’t really disagree. They’re three steps of one story.

Put them in order once, then use that exact line in the FAQ, the switching section and the sign-up area.

Built from the site’s own numbers. The team would confirm the real ones.

FIX 3

One list: live now, coming soon.

The same list in the AI menu, the Products menu and the homepage, and the badge matches what’s listed.

Then either merge Database Update and Candidate Update, or say in one line how they’re different.

FIXES 4 AND 5

Two clear doors in.

And the chat steps aside.

One button for people who want to try it, one for people who want to be shown. Each says who it’s for.

Tap the chat box to tuck it away

The chat folds into its icon with a small “Questions?” label. It’s still one tap away, and the page is readable again. Careers and bug bounty move to the footer.

Concept screen, drawn by me.

HOW I’D CHECK IT WORKED

  • How many homepage visitors start a trial or book a demo.
  • How many reach “Switch in days” and tap Talk to a migration specialist.
  • Whether chats go up or down once the box is tucked away.
